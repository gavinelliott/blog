I’ve had this personal space on the internet since 2006.

Back then, I created it to practise writing. My written English wasn’t good enough and needed to get better.

So what better way to get better than do it through writing?

And then over the years, as I moved through my career in design, I used it to share my thoughts and opinions on design.

If I found myself saying something multiple times in the ‘real world’, then I’d write about it here to share more easily with others.

It met a need, but often I wasn’t happy with the design.

And it’s been through quite a few.

Multiple different custom designs.

Multiple different WordPress themes (when it was WordPress).

But none of them really felt like ‘me’.

Reflecting on that, it could be down to the fact that I wasn’t being ‘me’. I was masking and presenting who I thought I should be, rather than the ‘real’ me.

I sometimes thought:

“What do I need to show to get _that_ job?”

“How do I show that I care about writing?”

“How do I represent my professionalism because I want to be a Design Director?”

It was always about how the audience would want to see something, or how I thought I needed to be seen.

Even since my autism diagnosis a few years ago, when I finally understood myself better than ever and realised that I didn’t need to mask or present a different version of myself, I still didn’t change anything.

Until now.

### Bringing diversity together

For the first time, I wanted a design that represented me, my likes, my mind and how I wanted it all to be seen.

For 20 years I’ve had ideas of where I wanted to take things.

Design that got halfway there.

Builds that never launched.

Ideas that never moved beyond a sketch.

Likes that were never shared.

Personality that was never truly represented.

> How the hell am I going to bring together my fascination for newspaper design, all of the random stuff I’ve done or am doing, Americana, the 80s, boldness, work, thoughts, the Thundercats and the colour ‘green’?

Well it turns out it can be done.

With freedom of thought, remembering that this is my space and a random Saturday.

So here’s the new version of my personal space on the internet.

I’ll continue to write, share books and now more of my personal projects because those things are what makes ‘me’, ‘me’.
