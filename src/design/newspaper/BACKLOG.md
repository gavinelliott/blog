## Rules for every screen

- Newspaper design, Gav’s voice. No invented habits or editorial filler.
- Preserve published article text when exploring layouts.
- Keep words intact. Change type size or layout rather than splitting letters across lines.
- Use real content and working navigation.
- Check mobile, intermediate and desktop widths.
- Keep preview routes out of normal production builds.
